the story

two strangers, one new home

Under a ten-year conservation agreement signed in April 2026, two six-year-old giant pandas are travelling roughly 8,000 miles from Chengdu to Atlanta. Ping Ping and Fu Shuang have never met. Their first introduction will happen at Zoo Atlanta, once both have cleared a quarantine of around 30 days. $熊猫 exists to celebrate that journey and to send real support to the people caring for them.
